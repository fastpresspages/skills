Privacy

Effective 26 Oct 2026. Plain language, complete list.

What this site collects

Nothing by itself. No cookies, no analytics, no localStorage, no third-party pixels. This page is served by GitHub Pages; GitHub's own infrastructure logging applies and is outside our control.

The contact form

The question/suggestion form on the front page sends two fields (email + message) plus metadata (page, date, request type) to meetcode99@gmail.com, delivered through formsubmit.co under their terms. Hidden honeypot field (_honey) exists to catch bots; real humans should never fill it. No submission list is stored anywhere else.

Fonts

This site loads Inter and Space Grotesk from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google when it does; no request ever leaves FastPress Labs.

Deletion or questions

Email meetcode99@gmail.com and we will delete what we hold (form submissions only) within a week.
